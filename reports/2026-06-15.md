### Testbirds
**Signal level:** 🔴 High
**URLs checked:** https://www.testbirds.com/en/, https://www.testbirds.com/en/services/, https://www.testbirds.com/en/services/testbirds-exclusives/
**Changes noted:**
- **June 8, 2026 — ACQUIRED BY UNGUESS.** UNGUESS (Italian crowdtesting platform) completed the acquisition of 100% of Testbirds GmbH and its European subsidiaries. Testbirds was the leading crowdtesting provider in DACH and Benelux, with offices in Germany, Netherlands, and UK. Round2 Capital (Testbirds backer since 2023) sold its shares; the deal was advised by Rittershaus law firm.
- The combined entity brings together UNGUESS's TRYBER tester community and Testbirds' "The Nest," creating a pool of 1M+ active testers across 100+ countries.
- Combined turnover: ~EUR 20M. Portfolio: 500+ enterprise clients including BMW, Generali, and Allianz.
- Geographic footprint: Italy, France, Germany, Netherlands, UK.
- Transaction supported by Fondo Italiano d'Investimento SGR (FITEC); P101 remains an UNGUESS investor.
- **Tracking note:** Testbirds and UNGUESS should now be tracked as a single combined entity going forward. Consider updating competitors.json.
