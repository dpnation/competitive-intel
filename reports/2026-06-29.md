### Qualitest → **QualityAI**
**Signal level:** 🔴 High
**URLs checked:** https://www.qualitestgroup.com/, https://www.qualitestgroup.com/solutions/, https://www.qualitestgroup.com/solutions/ai-testing/
**Changes noted:**
- **June 17, 2026 — Full corporate rebrand: Qualitest is now QualityAI.** Press release widely syndicated (Yahoo Finance, Investing.com, TradingView). Official announcement headline: *"Qualitest Becomes QualityAI: The Trusted Assurance and Engineering Partner Across Every Stage of Enterprise AI."*
- **New positioning:** Shifts from "software testing specialist" to "AI-first quality engineering partner." Emphasis on helping enterprises *design quality into complex systems from the outset* rather than bolt-on testing at the end.
- **New tagline/framing:** "QualityAI exists to give them that certainty, helping organizations move beyond AI claims and go live with confidence." (CEO Andrew Duncan)
- **Target verticals for rebrand:** Regulated industries — financial services, healthcare, energy, utilities, public sector. Positions AI assurance for high-stakes, compliance-driven deployment environments.
- **Scope of company as redefined:** Operates across data, models, platforms, infrastructure, and operational environments — a much broader remit than testing alone.
- **Continuity:** Built on ~30 years of software testing expertise; since 2019 has implemented proprietary AI solutions claimed to accelerate testing up to 6x. Flagship COCO tool and other proprietary products presumably continue under new brand.
