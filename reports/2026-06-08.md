### UserTesting
**Signal level:** 🟡 Medium
**URLs checked:** https://www.usertesting.com/, https://www.usertesting.com/plans, https://www.usertesting.com/platform
**Changes noted:**
- **June 3, 2026 — Research study released:** "Do software updates make our devices worse?" — commissioned study of 4,000 adults across U.S., U.K., and Australia (conducted April 8–14, 2026). Key findings: 56% feel anxious/annoyed before updating; 54% believe updates push users toward premium features; 40% need days to adapt; security improvements are the top motivator for installing updates. Widely picked up in regional/national press. Positions UserTesting as the authoritative voice on software experience research.
