## Competitor Updates

### Testlio
**Signal level:** 🔴 High
**URLs checked:** https://www.testlio.com/, https://www.testlio.com/news, https://www.testlio.com/blog/leocore-announcement, https://www.testlio.com/platform
**Changes noted:**
- **July 20, 2026:** Testlio unveiled **LeoCore™**, described as "the next evolution of its AI-driven testing platform." LeoCore is now positioned as the umbrella platform that orchestrates the entire testing lifecycle, with the previously-standalone LeoAI Engine™ repositioned as the intelligence layer inside it, powering LeoMatch™ (tester matching) and LeoInsights™ (analytics). This is a platform architecture/messaging change from the July 6 baseline, which described LeoAI Engine as the top-level branded technology.
- Alongside LeoCore, Testlio released its **2026 Software Quality Report**. Key findings: 34% of clients uncover at least one critical/high-severity defect in their first testing cycle with Testlio — breaking down to 44% functional bugs, 22% payment failures, 20% UX/usability, 14% other (localization, accessibility, regression). Separately, 77% of AI assistant failures tested were judged significant enough to damage user trust and product integrity.
- New customer proof point: Strava case study — 6x faster release velocity, 6–9 hour turnaround using real athletes testing across 13 global locations (quote from Benjamin Wolak, Mobile QA Engineer at Strava).
- New named executive quote: Dean Hickman-Smith, Chief Revenue Officer — not previously captured in baseline.
- Pricing FAQ (still no numbers published) now explicitly describes a two-part model: a platform fee (LeoCore access, tester matching, reporting, integrations, account management) plus an annual consumption fund (the actual testing work). This is new descriptive detail, not a public pricing page with figures.
- LeoCore also states MCP support for tool integrations (Jira, Slack, Azure DevOps, GitHub, mabl) — worth watching as an integration/dev-workflow angle.
