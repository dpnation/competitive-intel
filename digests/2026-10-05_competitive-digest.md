# Competitive Digest — 2026-10-05

## Summary

The busiest week on record for this scan — 4 High-signal developments, 9 Medium, 4 Low, and only one competitor (Appen) unchanged. The headline story is UserTesting's parent company rebranding as **Auros** (Sept 29), explicitly repositioning its 7.6M-person verified network toward AI training and evaluation data services — a direct conceptual overlap with Applause's own AI Evaluation Service. Two AI-eval acquisition storylines advanced: Dynatrace officially **closed** its $915M purchase of Arize AI (Oct 1, resolving the "pending" status carried since August), while Splunk/Cisco's Agent Observability (formerly Galileo) confirmed current post-Tokenomics pricing. Scale AI and Innodata each delivered significant, previously-unreported developments: Scale installed a new enterprise-veteran CEO (Francis deSouza, missed by an earlier scan window) alongside continued government/defense contract growth, while Innodata opened a motion-capture robotics lab and disclosed a potential ~$3B hyperscaler evaluation relationship. On the QA/testing side, three competitors picked up analyst or review-site recognition (QualityAI — Everest Group; Centific — Forrester; Level Access — G2 badges plus M-Enabling Summit presence), and two crowdtesting-heritage vendors — Global App Testing and UNGUESS — independently shifted homepage messaging toward "AI ground truth / data validation" language, worth watching as a converging positioning trend in the same space Applause competes in. Separately, QualityLogic surfaced a materially important compliance fact: the DOJ has pushed ADA Title II web-accessibility deadlines back roughly a year (large entities now April 2027, not April 2026).
