## Candidates Found

### Crux Labs
**Match type:** funding
**Why it matches:** Captures expert judgment from existing business workflows, builds custom evaluations for AI agents, and runs them against 100% of production traffic (human-judgment-based AI evaluation for enterprise deployers). Reports traction with Fortune 500 companies and high-growth firms in healthcare, cybersecurity and financial services. It appears software-led rather than a managed service with an external evaluator network, and traction claims are unverified.
