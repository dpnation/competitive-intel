# Discovery Scan — 2026-08-04

## Summary
Three candidates surfaced this week. The most notable is AudioEye (Nasdaq: AEYE) — a publicly traded accessibility platform combining AI detection, expert audits, and custom fixes that we haven't been tracking despite direct overlap with our accessibility testing positioning against Level Access and Fable.

## Candidates Found

### AudioEye
**Match type:** press
**Why it matches:** Public accessibility compliance platform unifying automated AI detection, expert (human) audits, and custom fixes in one system — a managed accessibility-testing-as-a-service model that competes directly with Level Access and Fable, both already tracked.
**Source:** [AudioEye Advances Next-Generation Digital Accessibility Platform](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/audioeye-advances-next-generation-digital-accessibility-platform-delivering-the-industrys-strongest-compliance-protection-302703561.html)

### Ubertesters
**Match type:** review-platform
**Why it matches:** Crowdsourced QA testing service with 120,000 vetted testers across 150 countries; fully managed testing cycles (functional, localization, payment, regression) run by an assigned project manager — a direct crowdtesting-marketplace competitor in the same category as Test IO and Testlio. Lists Applause itself as a top competitor per third-party tracking.
**Source:** [Ubertesters company profile](https://ubertesters.com/about-us/)

### AHEAD (managed penetration testing launch)
**Match type:** launch
**Why it matches:** IT services firm AHEAD launched an AI-powered managed penetration testing service in July 2026 offering continuous, human-validated offensive security testing for enterprises — fits the "security testing delivered as a service" criterion.
**Source:** [AHEAD Brings Continuous Validation to Enterprise Penetration Testing](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/30/3336113/0/en/AHEAD-Brings-Continuous-Validation-to-Enterprise-Penetration-Testing.html)

## Notes
Most of this week's funding/launch queries surfaced pure AI-native test automation startups (BotGauge AI, Momentic, ManaMind, Qase, QA.tech, Thunder Code, Functionize) — these are tooling plays with no managed human-testing layer, so they're excluded per the standing criteria. Also surfaced a long tail of generic QA outsourcing shops (e.g. TestFort) that are decades-old and undifferentiated; not flagging individually to avoid noise, but the category is worth a periodic sanity check. Separately, human-in-the-loop AI data/evaluation vendors (LXT, Welo Data) keep appearing alongside LMArena-style plays — this looks like an emerging adjacent category (data-annotation vendors repositioning as "AI evaluation") worth watching as a cluster rather than one-off candidates.
