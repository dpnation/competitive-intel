# Discovery Scan — 2026-10-06

## Summary
Two new candidates this week, both AI evaluation vendors: Vals AI, an independent AI evaluation platform that raised a $40M Series A at a $400M valuation (Aug 2026) with revenue up 8x, and Deccan AI, a human-expert post-training and evaluation services provider that raised a $25M Series A (Mar 2026). Vals AI is the most notable, given its funding momentum and its use by enterprise AI deployers to choose and compare models.

## Candidates Found

### Vals AI
**Match type:** funding
**Why it matches:** Independent AI evaluation platform that builds private-test-set benchmarks measuring whether AI models can do professional work (law, banking, engineering, medicine), with enterprise AI deployments using it to choose which models to build on. Raised a $40M Series A led by a16z at a $400M valuation (reported Aug 15, 2026); revenue is up 8x vs. all of 2025 and its customer base doubled. Adjacent to LMArena (already watchlisted) in the AI evaluation category. It leans toward benchmarking and measurement rather than managed human-in-the-loop testing, so treat it as lower confidence.
**Source:** [Vals AI Raises $40 Million Series A At $400 Million Valuation As Revenue Grows 8x](https://pulse2.com/vals-ai-raises-40-million-series-a-at-400-million-valuation-as-revenue-grows-8x/)

### Deccan AI
**Match type:** funding
**Why it matches:** Post-training data and evaluation services company (founded Oct 2024; $25M Series A led by A91 Partners, announced Mar 25, 2026). Runs expert-feedback, evaluation and red-teaming projects through a 1M+ contributor network (about 5,000-10,000 active monthly) and sells an evaluation suite, Helix, to enterprises. Customers include Google DeepMind and Snowflake. The same managed, human-expert-network evaluation model as Appen (tracked) and Welo Data (watchlisted). About 80% of revenue comes from its top five customers, mostly frontier AI labs, so it is not yet aimed at Applause's enterprise B2C verticals. The funding announcement is older than the ~30-day window but has not been flagged before.
**Source:** [Mercor competitor Deccan AI raises $25M, sources experts from India](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/25/deccan-ai-raises-25m-as-ai-training-push-relies-on-india-based-workforce)
