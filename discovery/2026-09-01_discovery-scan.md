# Discovery Scan — 2026-09-01

## Summary
Three candidates found this week, all in the AI red-teaming/evaluation category — the most notable is Mindgard, which closed a $30M Series A (Aug 12, 2026) for its AI Red Teaming as a Service offering, pairing automated adversarial testing with human security experts, a direct parallel to Applause's AI evaluation and human-in-the-loop validation services.

## Candidates Found

### Mindgard
**Match type:** funding
**Why it matches:** London/Boston-based AI security firm sells "AI Red Teaming as a Service" — automated red-teaming combined with human expert-led engagements (dedicated Technical Account Manager, AI Security Expert subscription). Closed a $30M Series A led by Album VC on 2026-08-12 (total funding ~$42M), citing strong enterprise demand. Fits the human-in-the-loop AI/Gen AI validation services criterion directly.
**Source:** [Mindgard Raises $30M Series A](https://mindgard.ai/blog/mindgard-raises-30m-series-a)

### Irregular
**Match type:** funding
**Why it matches:** Israeli frontier AI security lab running red-teaming, safety evaluations, and misuse testing on AI models before release, on behalf of AI labs and government. $80M raised, revenue from contracts with OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google DeepMind. Same AI-evaluation/red-teaming-as-a-service category as watchlisted LMArena and Armadin, though its buyers are model labs and government rather than enterprise B2C — flagging as lower-confidence fit given the buyer-profile mismatch.
**Source:** [Irregular company profile](https://dealroom.co/companies/irregular-1/)

### Aim Intelligence
**Match type:** funding
**Why it matches:** South Korea-based AI security company offering automated vulnerability detection for generative AI plus "specialized cybersecurity services" for AI red-teaming and guardrails. Raised 10B won (~$7.3M) Series A led by Samsung Venture Investment (announced April 2026, surfaced in this week's funding-news sweep). Smaller and more product-led than Mindgard — flagging as lower-confidence given the services layer looks secondary to the software license.
**Source:** [Aim Intelligence Series A](https://en.sedaily.com/technology/2026/04/10/ai-security-startup-aim-intelligence-raises-10-billion-won)

## Notes
AI red-teaming/evaluation continues to be the most active sub-category for new entrants and funding, consistent with prior weeks (LMArena, Armadin, Fabraix already on the watchlist). Traditional QA/crowdtesting and accessibility/payment/localization searches surfaced no new companies this week — mostly established players (Testbirds/UNGUESS merger news, BrowserStack, Global App Testing) and pure automation tools (Fire Your QA, Propolis) that don't meet the managed-service bar. Worth watching whether AI red-teaming keeps producing candidates faster than the other specialty-testing categories, and whether the buyer-profile criterion needs a carve-out for AI-lab-facing red-teaming vendors (Irregular, and arguably LMArena already on the watchlist) since they sit adjacent to Applause's AI evaluation business even though their primary buyer isn't enterprise B2C.
