# Discovery Scan — 2026-07-28

## Summary
Six candidates surfaced this week across AI evaluation, AI red-teaming, and crowdtesting/managed testing services; the most notable is LMArena, which closed a $150M round in January 2026 and now sells "AI Evaluations" as a paid service — enterprises and model labs hire its human community to assess model performance — a direct parallel to Applause's own AI evaluation offering.

## Candidates Found

### LMArena
**Match type:** funding
**Why it matches:** Sells AI Evaluations as a paid service — AI labs and enterprises (including OpenAI, Google, xAI) hire LMArena's community to evaluate model performance via human head-to-head comparisons. Annualized consumption run rate topped $30M within four months of launch. Directly overlaps Applause's AI evaluation / human-in-the-loop Gen AI validation line.
**Source:** [LMArena Raises $150 Million to Build the World's Most Trusted AI Evaluation Platform](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lmarena-raises-150-million-to-build-the-worlds-most-trusted-ai-evaluation-platform-302653012.html)

### Armadin
**Match type:** funding
**Why it matches:** Launched with $189.9M in seed/Series A funding for an AI-native red-teaming platform that pairs autonomous attack agents with human experts who validate findings and hunt novel techniques — a human-in-the-loop AI red-teaming service for large enterprises, adjacent to Applause's AI red-teaming/validation services.
**Source:** [Armadin Secures Record-Breaking $189.9M in Seed and Series A Funding](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/armadin-secures-record-breaking-189-9-million-in-seed-and-series-a-funding-to-combat-the-era-of-ai-driven-hyperattacks-302709318.html)

### TestParty
**Match type:** press
**Why it matches:** AI-enabled digital accessibility compliance platform that uses a human-in-the-loop model (CPACC-certified pros approve code fixes) rather than overlays — a managed accessibility testing/remediation service, same category as Level Access and Fable on our tracked list.
**Source:** [Accessibility Investment Landscape: VC Funding and M&A Activity](https://testparty.ai/blog/accessibility-investment-landscape)

### AppTestify
**Match type:** review-platform
**Why it matches:** On-demand, fully managed application testing service — 2,500+ verified testers plus 100+ in-house ISTQB-certified engineers delivering manual, automation, performance, accessibility, mobile and security testing. Same managed-crowdtesting model as Testlio/Test IO.
**Source:** [AppTestify Company Profile](https://superbcompanies.com/organizations/apptestify/)

### Digivante
**Match type:** review-platform
**Why it matches:** UK-based crowdtesting company running managed UAT plus real-user crowdtesting (functional, usability, accessibility, payment, UX) specifically for ecommerce brands — clients include Microsoft, Metro Bank, Audi and Specsavers. Strong overlap with Applause's ecommerce/retail buyer base.
**Source:** [Digivante — Expert CX Optimisation and Testing Services](https://www.digivante.com/)

### Crowdsprint
**Match type:** review-platform
**Why it matches:** Crowdsourced testing service (a Revolution IT company) with 100,000+ testers, fully managed by a dedicated Test and Delivery Manager, covering functional, accessibility, localization, security and UX testing. Also offers white-label crowdtesting for other providers to resell — worth watching as a potential downstream/OEM competitor as well as a direct one.
**Source:** [Crowdsprint — Crowdsourced Testing Services](https://crowdsprint.com/)
