# Discovery Scan — 2026-11-03

## Summary
Two new candidates found this week. Most notable: QA Wolf, a hybrid SaaS plus fully managed QA service (about $56M total funding) that is not yet tracked and overlaps Applause's managed functional and regression testing.

## Candidates Found

### QA Wolf
**Match type:** funding
**Why it matches:** Sells fully managed end-to-end QA as a service, with its own engineers building and maintaining automated test coverage for engineering teams (positions around guaranteed coverage). Series B of $36M led by Scale Venture Partners (July 2024), about $56M total per aggregators. Not a new company, but it has not appeared in competitors.json, watchlist.json or any prior scan. Funding totals come from aggregators and are unverified.
**Source:** [QA Wolf on VCBacked](https://www.vcbacked.co/company/qa-wolf)

### Intelligence (maker of Design Arena)
**Match type:** funding
**Why it matches:** Raised a $7.9M seed led by Index Ventures (TechCrunch, 2026-08-03). Runs human head-to-head comparison of AI outputs (Design Arena, claimed 5.3M users) and sells human-judged AI evaluation, a model similar to LMArena (already watchlisted). Press relays a $60M ARR claim, which is unverified. Customer base appears to be AI model developers, so enterprise B2C overlap is limited.
**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/03/designarena-creators-raise-7-9-million-to-bring-taste-to-ai-models/)
