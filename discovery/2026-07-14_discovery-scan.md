# Discovery Scan — 2026-07-14

## Summary
Five candidates surfaced this week, none currently in competitors.json or watchlist.json. The most notable is LMArena, which raised $150M in January 2026 (now at a $1.7B valuation) for paid AI evaluation services — though its primary buyers are AI labs rather than Applause's typical enterprise B2C target, so it's a borderline fit worth watching rather than an immediate priority.

## Candidates Found

### TestParty
**Match type:** funding
**Why it matches:** AI-powered accessibility testing and remediation platform (auto-rewrites source code for WCAG/ADA compliance) sold as a service to enterprises — direct overlap with Applause's accessibility testing line (competes alongside Level Access and Fable). Raised a $4M seed co-led by Harlem Capital and Urban Innovation Fund.
**Source:** [TestParty Raises $4M to Make Building Accessible Websites Seamless with AI](https://www.alleywatch.com/2024/07/testparty-ai-powered-accessibility-testing-platform-for-websites-compliance-monitoring-michael-bervell/)

### QualiBooth
**Match type:** funding
**Why it matches:** Spanish/European accessibility testing and compliance platform targeting enterprise brands and retailers ahead of the European Accessibility Act; AI-powered testing suite integrated into dev workflows. Raised €2M to fund European expansion.
**Source:** [QualiBooth secures €2M to accelerate expansion as European accessibility legislation takes effect](https://tech.eu/2025/07/08/qualibooth-secures-2m-to-accelerate-european-expansion-as-accessibility-legislation-takes-effect/)

### Testeum
**Match type:** review-platform
**Why it matches:** Crowdtesting marketplace model — connects a curated global tester community (5,000+) to run functional, exploratory and usability testing for digital agencies, ecommerce companies and app developers. Core business model overlap with Applause's real-world testing category.
**Source:** [Testeum — Software, UX & User crowd testing platform](https://testeum.com/)

### LXT
**Match type:** community
**Why it matches:** Offers human-in-the-loop services explicitly including "model validation" and evaluating AI model outputs post-deployment, backed by a 10M+ contributor crowd (via clickworker). Established company (founded 2010) not previously on Applause's tracked list; overlaps with the AI evaluation/human-in-the-loop validation criteria, though its core business is broader AI training data rather than enterprise software testing.
**Source:** [LXT — Human-in-the-Loop Services for AI Model Development](https://www.lxt.ai/services/human-in-the-loop-services/)

### LMArena
**Match type:** funding
**Why it matches:** Raised $150M (Jan 2026, $1.7B valuation) selling paid AI evaluation services, with annualized consumption run rate over $30M within four months of launch. Borderline fit — its buyers are primarily AI labs and model builders, not enterprise B2C brands — but the funding scale and speed of monetization make it worth monitoring as the AI evaluation category matures.
**Source:** [AI evaluation startup LMArena raises $150M at $1.7B valuation](https://siliconangle.com/2026/01/06/ai-evaluation-startup-lmarena-raises-150m-1-7b-valuation/)
