# Discovery Scan — 2026-08-25

## Summary
Three candidates found this week: two heavily-funded AI-native penetration-testing-as-a-service entrants (Terra Security, XBOW) and one established crowdtesting marketplace (Testeum) not previously on either list. Terra Security is the most notable — it explicitly brands as "Penetration Testing-as-a-Service," uses human-in-the-loop oversight, and already counts Fortune 500 customers.

## Candidates Found

### Terra Security
**Match type:** funding
**Why it matches:** Agentic-AI-powered continuous penetration testing platform explicitly marketed as "Penetration Testing-as-a-Service" with human-in-the-loop oversight layered on autonomous AI agents. Raised $30M Series A (Felicis, Dell Technologies Capital, SVCI) plus earlier seed funding; Fortune 500 customers and an AWS partnership. Same security-testing-as-a-service category as AHEAD and Fabraix, both already watchlisted.
**Source:** [Terra Security raises $30M Series A](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/awdq1yv5k)

### XBOW
**Match type:** funding
**Why it matches:** Autonomous offensive-security/pentesting platform that raised $120M at a $1B+ valuation (Sequoia, DFJ Growth, Northzone participating), $237M total raised. Sold to enterprises as continuous, AI-driven exploit validation rather than a one-time tool license — same security-testing-as-a-service category as Terra Security, AHEAD, and Fabraix. Leans more autonomous/less explicitly human-in-the-loop than Terra, so worth watching for how "managed" the delivery model becomes rather than treating it as a confirmed services peer yet.
**Source:** [XBOW raises $120M at $1B+ valuation](https://www.securityweek.com/autonomous-offensive-security-firm-xbow-raises-120m-at-1b-valuation/)

### Testeum
**Match type:** review-platform
**Why it matches:** Established (Australia/New Caledonia-based) crowdtesting marketplace connecting mobile/web app creators with a vetted tester community, offering both self-service campaigns and expert-managed testing. Same crowdtesting-marketplace model as Test IO, Ubertesters, and Digivante — not previously on competitors.json or watchlist.json.
**Source:** [testeum.com](https://testeum.com/)

## Notes
AI-native penetration testing is now the most active adjacent category surfacing in these scans (Terra Security and XBOW this week, following AHEAD and Fabraix in prior weeks) — worth a standalone security-testing-as-a-service mini-map alongside the accessibility one flagged last week. General "AI evaluation platform" and "Gen AI evaluation" searches this week surfaced only research orgs (NIST, METR) and cloud-vendor tooling (Google Vertex AI, AWS Bedrock AgentCore) with no new managed-service entrants. Localization and UX-research searches surfaced no new qualifying candidates — dominated by incumbent tooling (Lokalise, Smartling) and design agencies with no crowdtesting/panel-as-a-service layer.
