# Discovery Scan — 2026-07-21

## Summary
Two candidates found this week. The most notable is TestParty, an AI-powered accessibility testing and remediation platform combining automated detection with human-in-the-loop expert fixes — a direct model match to Applause's real-world testing approach, just narrower in scope (accessibility only).

## Candidates Found

### TestParty
**Match type:** funding
**Why it matches:** AI-driven accessibility testing platform that pairs automated WCAG detection with human-in-the-loop expert remediation (delivered via GitHub PRs) — matches the "specialized testing as a service: accessibility" and "human-in-the-loop AI validation" criteria. Founded 2023, raised a $4M seed (Harlem Capital, Urban Innovation Fund, Soma Capital, NSF SBIR); customers span enterprise sites. Overlaps with Level Access and Fable, already tracked, but worth watching as a funded, growing entrant.
**Source:** [TestParty Raises $4M — AlleyWatch](https://www.alleywatch.com/2024/07/testparty-ai-powered-accessibility-testing-platform-for-websites-compliance-monitoring-michael-bervell/), [testparty.ai](https://testparty.ai/)

### Fabraix
**Match type:** funding
**Why it matches:** YC-backed (founded 2026) AI red-teaming company; its agent "Nyx" continuously probes customer-facing AI agents for security vulnerabilities and has surfaced issues at dozens of Fortune 500 companies — fits the "AI red-teaming" criterion explicitly called out in our match list. Caveat: Nyx reads as a self-serve automated agent rather than a human-in-the-loop managed service, so it sits closer to the tool/service line than TestParty does — flagging for awareness rather than a clean-fit recommendation.
**Source:** [Fabraix — Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/fabraix), [fabraix.com](https://fabraix.com/)

## Notes
- Searches on payment testing, localization testing, and UX research services surfaced only established players (Testlio, Test IO, FIS, Great Question, Nielsen Norman Group, etc.) or pure dev-tooling (Applitools, Tolgee, Lingo.dev) — nothing new enough or service-layered enough to qualify.
- The AI agentic-QA space (TestSprite, QA.tech, Cekura, Quash, AgenticQA) is crowded with 2026 Product Hunt launches, but nearly all are self-serve automation tools without a managed/human-in-the-loop service layer — excluded per criteria. Worth revisiting if any of these add a managed-service tier.
- "AI evaluation" as a funding category is heating up (41% of AI-safety capital despite only 10% of deal count per one analysis) — expect more candidates here in coming weeks as more of these platforms add human-in-the-loop or managed offerings.
